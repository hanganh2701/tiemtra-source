Download TikTok photos (slideshows)

Paste a TikTok photo post link to download each original photo, the whole set in a ZIP file, or turn the photos and music into a video.

Photo post links usually look like tiktok.com/@name/photo/… or a short link like vt.tiktok.com/…

Download TikTok slideshow photos

  1. Copy the photo post link

    Open the photo post on TikTok and tap Share › Copy link.

  2. Paste it in the box above

    The site shows every photo in the post, in the original order.

  3. Choose how to download

    Download photos one by one, the whole set (.zip), or tap Turn photos into a video to get a video with music.

Photo post FAQ

Do downloaded photos have a watermark?

No. These are the original photos the creator uploaded, with no TikTok logo.

How do I open a ZIP file on my phone?

Android: open the Files app and tap the ZIP file › Extract. iPhone: open the Files app and tap the ZIP file; it unzips into a folder automatically.

How does turning photos into a video work?

Your browser puts it together on its own: each photo shows for 2.5 seconds with a fade between them, and the original music plays in the background. It runs in real time (a 10-photo post takes about 25 seconds), so keep the page open until it's done. Recent versions of Chrome and Safari make an MP4; other browsers may make a WebM.

Can't open a photo ending in .webp?

snaptik prefers the JPEG version and only downloads WebP when TikTok has no JPEG. Modern computers and phones can all open WebP.
