Download TikTok audio as MP3

Save just the original audio of a TikTok video or photo post as a music file. Paste the link, then tap Download MP3 audio.

Works with both video links and links to photo posts with music.

Get the music from TikTok

  1. Copy the post link

    Open the video with the song you like and tap Share › Copy link.

  2. Paste it in the box above

    Tap Paste: the site finds the post and shows the track name, the creator and the length.

  3. Tap Download MP3 audio

    The audio file is saved to your Downloads folder and plays in any music player.

TikTok audio FAQ

Which audio do I get?

The original sound attached to the TikTok post: either the song the creator picked, or the sound recorded in the video itself.

Why is there no Download MP3 audio button on some posts?

For some copyrighted songs, TikTok doesn't let you get the audio file on its own. You can still download the video, then use a video editing app to extract the sound.

Why is the file .m4a instead of .mp3?

snaptik keeps the exact file TikTok sends and doesn't convert it, so the quality doesn't drop. Most files are MP3; if it's M4A, phones and computers still open it just fine.

How can I use downloaded music the right way?

Music is copyrighted by its songwriters, singers or recording owners. Listening on your own is fine; putting it in a public video or using it to make money needs permission. See the Terms.
