Download TikTok videos without watermark

Paste a TikTok link and the cat fetches you an MP4 without watermark, the HD version, MP3 music or slideshow photos. Free, no ads, no sign-up.

On your phone: open TikTok › Share › Copy link, then come back here and tap Paste.

Download TikTok videos in three steps

  1. Copy the link

    In the TikTok app, open the video, tap Share and choose Copy link. On a computer, copy the address from the browser bar.

  2. Paste it in the box

    Come back to snaptik and tap Paste (or long-press the box and choose Paste). The site finds the video right away.

  3. Pick a download

    Tap Download without watermark, Download HD video or Download MP3 audio. The file is saved to your device's Downloads folder.

Private

Frequently asked questions

Does snaptik cost anything?

No. It's free, with no ads and no sign-up or login.

Do downloaded videos still have the TikTok watermark?

No. snaptik grabs the version TikTok plays on the web, which has no logo or username on it.

How do I download on iPhone?

Use Safari, tap a download button and choose Download. The file goes to the Files app › Downloads. Open it and tap Share › Save Video to put it in the Photos app. More on the Guide page.

Where do downloaded videos go?

Android: the Download folder (open it with the Files app or Gallery). Computer: the Downloads folder, or whichever folder you chose in your browser.

Why won't some videos download?

Usually the video is private, deleted or region-blocked, or TikTok is temporarily blocking our server. Check that the link opens on TikTok, then try again in a few minutes.

Can I download videos from private accounts?

No. snaptik can only read what anyone can see on TikTok without logging in.

Can I download Stories?

Maybe. An active Story (under 24 hours old) from a public account has a link just like a video link, so pasting it might work, but snaptik hasn't been tested with Stories. Once a Story expires, it can't be downloaded.

Can I repost videos I download?

A video belongs to the person who made it. Only download for personal viewing, or with the owner's permission. If you want to repost, ask first and credit the source. See the Terms.

Is snaptik made by TikTok or SnapTik?

No. snaptik at snaptik.meomeo.app is a small site made for fun, looked after by a cat. It isn't affiliated with TikTok, ByteDance or any other site called SnapTik.
